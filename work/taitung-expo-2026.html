The Brief專案概念

2026 台東博覽會《未來人孵化器》教育展的宣傳動畫。

2050 年的未來人「東東」意外降落在 2026 年的台東，帶觀眾走進教育館。

體內流動的光球、逐漸被點亮的色彩與不斷變化的圖形，把適應、理解、思考與共創化為能量。

A promo animation for the Future Human Incubator education pavilion at the 2026 Taitung Expo.

Dongdong, a visitor from the year 2050, lands in Taitung in 2026 and leads the audience into the pavilion.

Glowing orbs flowing inside Dongdong, colours lighting up and shapes that keep changing turn adapting, understanding, thinking and co-creating into energy.
