The Brief專案概念

跟著音樂影像，我們沈浸在不同溫度的故事中，體驗超越現實的感官敘事。

以冬日打扮的聽者為中心，音樂的熱浪快速擴散，超現實角色和熱帶物件一同包圍改變聽者身處的環境和季節。

Following the music and visuals, we immerse ourselves in stories of varying temperatures, experiencing sensory narratives beyond reality.

With a listener adorned in winter attire at the center, the heatwaves of music rapidly spread, surrounding the listener with surreal characters and tropical objects, transforming the environment and season in which the listener resides.
