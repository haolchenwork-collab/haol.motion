2023 Taichung International Animation Festival (TIAF)

2023 台中國際動畫影展

Promote / 2023 / Mixcode Studio/ Role 2D Motion、3D Motion(0s-06s)、Compositing

「萬物都有裂縫，那是光照進來的地方」，光影之間是力量的延伸，就有無限的希望可能，啄木鳥領路，探索城市中每一個光點，創作者靈魂隱身在城市裡的角落。

Everything has cracks, that's how the light gets in." Between light and shadow lies the extension of power, with endless possibilities of hope. The woodpecker leads the way, exploring every glimmer in the city, while the creator's soul hides in the corners of the urban landscape.
