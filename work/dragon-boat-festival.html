Dragon Boat Festival (Nulls Design)

端午節

Creative / 2021 / Nulls Design / Role Concept、Motion、Compositing

在端午節，競爭是主要活動，以兩艘船互相競爭的方式，爭奪美味的粽子。最終的結果是不分上下，營造一種不分勝負的氛圍。場景方面，我們選擇使用一些典型的端午節元素，以使整個場景更具節日氛圍。

The Dragon Boat Festival primarily revolves around competition, and we have chosen to illustrate this by depicting two boats competing to seize zongzi. Ultimately, there is no clear winner, creating a sense of equality. The scene is designed to be festive, incorporating some common elements of the Dragon Boat Festival.
