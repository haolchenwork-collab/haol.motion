Golden Melody Award 33st Best Hakka Female Vocalist

金曲33 最佳客語女歌手獎

Award / 2022 / Mixcode Studio / Role Motion、Compositing

客語歌曲有著獨特的豐富聲調，渲染力極強，宛如人體蓄積的能量向外爆發擴散帶來改變整個環境的熱度，不同曲風的碰撞更為音樂市場帶來嶄新的可能。

Hakka songs possess a unique and rich tonality, with a powerful rendering force that feels like the accumulated energy within a human body exploding and spreading outward. They bring about a fervor that changes the entire environment, and the collision of different musical styles introduces fresh possibilities to the music market.
