THE BRIEF
專案概念

以「蛇行祈福之路」為主題，帶領觀眾俯瞰這片土地，重新詮釋燈籠溫潤的火光，將溫暖色調與光影融入畫面。

Under the theme of 'The Serpentine Path of Blessings,' we guide the audience to gaze down upon this land, reimagining the gentle glow of lanterns. Warm tones and the interplay of light and shadow are artfully woven into the scene.
