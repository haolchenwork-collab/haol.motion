THE BRIEF · 專案概念

以貓咪善於爬樹、小狗擅長游泳的特性作為分工的舉例，視覺上也採用分割畫面的方式，讓分工的選擇、產值的比較在演繹上更為直觀，同時說明企業如何透過專業分工，提升社會的生產力並創造價值

Using the examples of cats excelling in tree climbing and dogs being proficient swimmers as a demonstration of division of labor, we also employ a visually segmented approach. This allows for a more intuitive presentation of the selection of specialized tasks and the comparison of output. Additionally, we elucidate how businesses can enhance societal productivity and create value through professional specialization.
