THE BRIEF
專案概念

這部作品以獨特而誇張的省錢方法為主軸，呈現了一家人的生活點滴與夢想。通過這些誇張的省錢場景，體會到家庭生活中的溫馨與歡樂。

在動畫以紙為核心元素，來建構居家環境，營造出溫馨、和樂、充滿夢想氛圍。

This work revolves around unique and exaggerated cost-saving methods, portraying the daily lives and dreams of a family. Through these exaggerated frugal scenes, the audience experiences the warmth and joy of family life. In the animation, paper serves as a central element to construct the home environment, creating a cozy, harmonious, and dream-filled atmosphere.
