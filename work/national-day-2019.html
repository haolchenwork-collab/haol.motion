Taiwan Nation Day (2019)

​2019國慶日

Promote / 2019 / 27 Design / Role Motion(23s-40s)

如箭頭般往右上前進的雙十符號，象徵台灣追求進步的腳步不曾停歇。 好，還要更好。

The Double Ten symbol, advancing like an arrow pointing upward and to the right, symbolizes Taiwan's relentless pursuit of progress, never ceasing in its steps. Good, and even better. Keep going.
