THE BRIEF · 專案概念

台語文化裡的那份在地與草根，總是用生命力最強的方式真實唱出每個人在心裡最底層的那份感觸，而在世俗中伴隨著他們的歌聲，生活似乎就也沒這麼辛苦。

In the rich tapestry of Taiwanese culture, the essence of locality and grassroots sentiments is often authentically expressed in the most vibrant manner. Through the powerful medium of their songs, individuals convey the deepest emotions residing in their hearts. Accompanied by these voices in the midst of the mundane, life seems to lose some of its harshness.
