The Brief專案概念

SPARK 不只是一場活動，而是串起世代、情感與記憶的象徵。

以俐落的無襯線字體，呈現台北 101 作為國際地標的姿態。

中心 A 字的光暈流動象徵火花綻放，從 11 月閃耀到隔年 2 月。

SPARK is more than an event. It is a symbol that connects generations, emotions and memories.

A clean sans-serif typeface presents Taipei 101 as an international landmark.

A flowing glow at the centre of the “A” marks the moment a spark ignites, shining from November to February.
