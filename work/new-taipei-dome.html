The Brief專案概念

新北大巨蛋不只是一座場館，更像市民生活的未來藍圖。

以「文化、生活、創新、活力、永續」為核心，用動畫串起清晨展望、熱血競技、藝文綻放、築夢未來四段場景。

呼應「新北花園城市」，以葉片、花卉串連，角色轉場貼近鏡頭，在柔和氛圍裡注入生命力。

The New Taipei Dome is more than a venue. It is a blueprint for the future of city life.

Built on five core values (culture, lifestyle, innovation, vitality and sustainability), the animation moves through four scenes: morning outlook, passionate competition, arts in bloom and building the future.

Echoing New Taipei's "Garden City" image, leaves and flowers tie the scenes together, and characters transition close to the camera to bring energy into a soft atmosphere.
