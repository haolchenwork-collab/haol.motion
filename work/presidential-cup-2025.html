The Brief專案概念

「全民主場，等你上場！」以開放與參與感，向所有人發出號召。

打破專業運動員的視角與門檻，聚焦民間各處蓄勢待發的動能。

以充滿爆發力的標準字與全動畫影像，捕捉全民上場的瞬間。

We Play Bold. A call for everyone to take the stage at the Presidential Cup.

The focus shifts from professional athletes to the raw, latent energy of the people.

Explosive typography and fluid full animation capture the moment anyone steps up.
